# Output gate

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-11 so the file every session loads stays short; CLAUDE.md keeps a summary and points here. This is the full reference: every check `validate_assistant_reply()` and `sanitize_reply()` run on a reply before the customer sees it. Every rule carries the reason and the date it was added. Keep it current with `bot.py`: a new rule gets its line here, with why and when.

**The output gate: replies are checked before the customer sees them** — every response, tool-calling ones included (their tools haven't run yet, so regenerating them is safe). `sanitize_reply()` fixes mechanical issues (headings, `---`, extra emoji, common Czech words in Slovak sessions) and `_canonicalize_consent_question()` rewrites any consent question to the canonical wording. `validate_assistant_reply()` then sorts violations: **HARD** ones sit inside one sentence (`_HARD_RULES`: an amount `quote` didn't produce, `~`/`cca`/`approximately` before an amount, an invented model code, a permit promise, a question where the outdoor unit goes, a budget-fit claim no `quote` budget check supports (`_budget_claim_unsupported`: the quote of the product the sentence names, else the offer's last two; a denial — "nezmestí sa", "zatiaľ nemôžem potvrdiť, že sa zmestí" (`_BUDGET_NEGATION_RE`, A/B of 2026-11-09) — is no claim; what the budget covers — "Montáž je v rozpočte tiež zahrnutá", "rozpočet zahŕňa aj montáž" — is no claim, `_BUDGET_SCOPE_RE`; for an all-in budget `_budget_note` gives the sentence to say, with "jednotky" in it), internal reasoning or tool names shown to the customer, a callback promise or "zaznamenal som" / "údaje sme zaznamenali" before consent (`slub-bez-suhlasu`, `_premature_promise_sentences`; a condition on the contact or the consent passes, as the directives ask — "Po jej odsúhlasení vás kontaktuje…", "Akonáhle ho potvrdíte…", "Ak mi potvrdíte otázku nižšie…", "Once you confirm, …" — and so do a purpose clause "Aby sme žiadosť odovzdali…", a denial "nesľubujem, že…" and "potvrdí vám to PM pri obhliadke", `_PROMISE_EXEMPT_RE`; since 2026-11-08 also "po jej zadaní a výslovnom súhlase…" (words between "po" and "súhlase"), a "Ten sa vám potom ozve…" / "Potom…" right after a conditional sentence (`_CONTINUES_RE`), and a reply to what the customer writes to us — the company e-mail, "napíšte nám", "pri správe…" in the sentence or the one before, with "ozve / odpovie" and no call or recording claim (`_WRITE_TO_US_RE`, `_REPLY_PROMISE_RE`); since the API run of e695916 also "…, potvrdí vám projektový manažér" and the customer's question echoed as a heading, "Zavolá vám niekto?" (`_ECHOED_CALLBACK_RE`); "keď potvrdíte výber, ozveme sa" names a choice and stays a promise; about half the rewrites of the A/B of 2026-11-07 were such conditions), a promised delivery/installation date or a judgement that the customer's date is realistic, a quote amount shown under another product's name or colour (`cena-iny-produkt`, `_misattributed_amounts`; `_quote_identity` keeps `finishes` per line, because the outdoor unit is shared by a series' colours, and the colour named last before the amount counts: DAISEIKAI 10 Wood was shown at the White price), an amount labelled with the wrong DPH basis (`dph-zamena`, `_vat_mislabeled_amounts`; in "Cena s DPH: X (bez DPH Y)" the bracket's label is Y's), a catalog code called "not in the price list" (`mimo-katalogu`), SEER/SCOP/dB figures no tool returned or savings / payback claims, with or without a figure — "ušetrí peniaze na elektrine", "nižšie náklady na elektrinu" (`nepodlozene-udaje`, `_SAVINGS_CLAIM_RE`), a claim to be a person (`ai-identita`, `_HUMAN_CLAIM_RE`; "nie som človek" is fine), an energy class the price list doesn't give the named series, or a filtration claim against `filtration_policy.json` — a tier 1–2 series with a "štandardný / základný filter", a tier 2+ one with "najlepšia filtrácia" (`nepodlozene-udaje` too, `_wrong_energy_classes`, `_wrong_filtration_claims`; `_series_fragments` reads a heading as naming the series for the list under it and a table cell by its column's header; a flagged table row is cut whole; one class "na chladenie aj vykurovanie" must hold for both modes, while several classes on one line — "A++ na chladenie / A+ na vykurovanie", lookup_product's own wording for SEIYA Classic 2, rejected until 2026-10-28 — each only have to exist — AR35 was called "E/D", Avant S2 got "Štandardný filter" in a table), a colour no price list has, e.g. pink, unless negated or restating the customer's wish, a colour "at the same price", or "the documents list no colours" (`farba`, `_color_claims`), a comparison pointing the wrong way — "A je o X € lacnejší ako B" / "oproti B" / "A lacnejší než B o X €" when A's quote is the dearer one (`porovnanie-smer`, `_wrong_comparisons`, clause by clause, so a sentence with a third series still counts; the product after ako / oproti / než is the reference; a clause naming one product, "Oproti setom AR35 je tento variant lacnejší o X €", is read through the quote pair that differs by exactly X, `_one_name_backwards`), a difference that isn't the gap between totals the reply shows, "lacnejšie o X €" where X was one set against two (`porovnanie-suma`, `_wrong_differences`: amounts after "o", with two or more totals shown; a product not shown, an earlier offer or a discount compares with something else), "tichší" or "výkonnejší" about a named product or option ("druhá") with no dB or kW figure in the sentence (`porovnanie-bez-cisel`, `_unbacked_comparatives`: 2.5 kW was called stronger than 2.6 kW and a customer chose on a false "quieter"; "ak chcete…" exempts its own clause only), a wall-only series called a cassette (`typ-jednotky`, `_wall_called_cassette`), a brand's trademark in a sentence about the other brand (`brand-funkcia`, `_brand_term_mixups`: WindFree / Tri-care / Freeze Wash next to a Toshiba series, HADA / plasma ionizer / Ultra Pure next to a Samsung one; sentences naming both brands and negations pass; a brand written right before the other brand's series — "Samsung DAISEIKAI" — too), a feature only other series list in the price lists — plazmový ionizátor, Ultra Pure, HADA, textilný kryt, Freeze Wash, radar… — given to a series (`funkcia-radu`, `_wrong_feature_claims`: SEIYA got HAORI's filters), a statement about a multi-split's price while none was quoted — "mám ju nacenenú", "zvyčajne lacnejší" (`nenacenene`, `_unpriced_multi_claims`), a stock claim — "skladom", "hneď dostupný", "štandardne dostupný" — for a product whose quote `availability` isn't stock, or before any quote for Samsung, whose availability the PM confirms (`sklad-tvrdenie`, `_false_stock_claims`: the product named in the sentence, else its paragraph), a health effect — pain, a stiff neck, a cold, "zdravší", "vylieči" — unless the sentence refers to a doctor or declines (`zdravie-tvrdenie`, `_health_claim_sentences`), a free repair, replacement or service — "oprava je bezplatná", "bezplatný servis"; "bezplatná obhliadka" is fine (`zaruka-slub`, `_free_repair_promises`), a promise of a later message — "pošlem vám v ďalšej správe"; the bot can't write on its own — or of something nobody offers, "prepojiť s predajcom", "ukázať reálne kusy" (`neskorsia-sprava`, `_LATER_MESSAGE_RE`), the bot's own process, in English too — "the tool did not accept", "the customer's last message", "Čo viem spoľahlivo", "nacenenie nefunguje", "skúsim to znova", a made-up price list outage — "ďalšie volanie cenníka", "cenník vyhodnotil", "keď bude cenník dostupný" — "systém odmieta", "Lookup potvrdil", "the system keeps rejecting", "Tu systém kontroluje veľkosť" ("multi-split systém" is fine), any snake_case field name (not an e-mail address), a filtration tier by number (`interne-info`, `_INTERNAL_LEAK_RE`, `_internal_leak_sentences`; a phrase the customer quoted in the latest message — "čo znamená 'čo viem spoľahlivo'?" — is the answer's subject, not a leak: 5 of 8 in the API run of 9657449), a claim that a diagnosis was noted for the PM (`zdravie-zaznam`, `_health_noted_claims`), or a claim that installation is in the price (`montaz-v-cene`, `_INSTALL_INCLUDED_RE`)). A sentence opening with a pronoun that pointed at a cut one ("Jeho cena je…") goes with it (`_ORPHAN_START_RE`). They get up to `MAX_REGENS_PER_TURN` (2) regenerations per customer turn with per-rule instructions (`_regen_note`, never "keep the same content"); whatever still violates is cut out sentence by sentence (`_drop_hard_sentences`). **REGEN-ONLY** problems cover the whole reply (`_REGEN_ONLY_RULES`: a reply largely in Czech; a direct price question left without a quote although the size or the exact codes are known; a request for contact details or the town before any price, `kontakt-pred-ponukou`; an empty final reply when nothing was shown this turn, `prazdna-odpoved`; the conversation's first offer showing one priced option when the customer named no model or series, `dve-moznosti`, `_single_first_offer` — the owner wants two to choose from, and like `cena-neodpovedana` its regeneration must call a tool; an English reply, `jazyk-anglictina` (`_english_slip`; since 2026-11-09 to every customer, an English-writing one too — the bot answers only in Slovak, the owner's decision; if it survives the regenerations it is replaced by `_EMPTY_REPLY_FALLBACK`, `english_reply_replaced`); a price put off on an offer move — "ak ju chcete, pripravím ju", "zatiaľ som nenacenil" (`_PRICE_LATER_RE`) — or an offer move with no quote this turn and no amount in the reply ("Dajte mi chvíľu…"), `cena-neodpovedana`, whose regeneration must call a tool; a question about the preferred brand at any time (once both brands were quoted, "Samsung alebo Toshiba?" is a choice between the offers and passes — the owner, 2026-11-08), or before the first quote about the budget once the size is known, `zakazana-otazka`, `_forbidden_questions` — its regeneration must call a tool when the size is known, and a question that survives the regenerations is cut; a price question whose quote was made this turn but whose price the reply doesn't state, `cena-nezobrazena`; tykanie to a customer who never tykal — no "ahoj", no 2nd-person forms (`vykanie`, `_customer_tyka`, `_TYKANIE_RE`; English sessions skipped; tykanie mixed with vykanie in one reply stays soft); a technology the customer asks about by name — WindFree, HADA, plazmový ionizátor… (`_TECH_TERM_RES`) — described in the reply while no tool result of the conversation names it, `funkcia-bez-podkladu`, whose regeneration must call a tool — except technologies no search can name: those the price lists and rules never mention (`_KB_ABSENT_TECH`, computed at startup; today Siri and HomeKit) and photovoltaics, which the server context answers up front instead (`_TECH_ANSWERED_BY_SERVER`; the live check of 2026-10-27 regenerated photovoltaics twice and still got a lecture on inverters); two or more distinct misspelled words in a Slovak reply, `pravopis` — at most one regeneration per turn, what is left after it is logged as soft `pravopis-slovo`, and so is a single word (`_misspelled_words`: not in sk-spell's dictionary read with `spylls`, nor in the words of `knowledge/` and `config/`, `dictionaries/klimabot_sk.txt`, the company name or the customer's own words; short, ALL-CAPS and CamelCase tokens skipped, brand names in Slovak cases — "Samsungu", "Toshibe" — and naj- superlatives accepted, mostly-English replies skipped; on the logs of 2026-10-27 a quarter of the demo's replies would regenerate once — add a correct word that shows up in a `pravopis` event to `klimabot_sk.txt`); an amount corrected inside the reply — "24 597,54 €... presnejšie podľa ponuky: 23 479,47 €" (`samooprava`, `_SELF_CORRECTION_RE`); "the cheapest" when every quote of the turn carries a warning that it isn't, or a quote for as many units costs less (`najlacnejsie-tvrdenie`, `_false_cheapest_claim`, `_wrong_cheapest_sentences`; its regeneration must call a tool, and after the last one the label in brackets is removed); a multi-split called the better deal with no dearer separate sets quoted for as many units (`oplati-sa-tvrdenie`, `_false_multi_value_claim`; its regeneration must call a tool); tykanie mixed with vykanie for a customer who tyká (`oslovenie-mix`)) — regenerated, never cut. A final reply that is still empty gets `_EMPTY_REPLY_FALLBACK`: the customer never gets silence. After a cut, lines left with only a list marker, emoji or punctuation and a closing heading are removed (`_tidy_after_cut`). On the discovery moves a sentence with a kW figure is cut unless the customer typed one (`_drop_kw_sentences`, `kw_sentence_dropped`), and so is one with an area the customer never typed — "Dvojizbový byt býva zvyčajne 50 až 65 m²" to a customer who didn't know the size, every version in the A/B of 2026-11-06 (`_drop_invented_m2`, `m2_sentence_dropped`; the customer's numbers and the total of their areas stay). An approximation marker in front of an amount `quote` returned exactly is simply removed (`_strip_exact_approx`) instead of costing a regeneration. Everything else is **SOFT**: fixed mechanically where safe (soft hyphens and zero-width spaces removed — "vykonfigu­rovať" read as two words; missing diacritics in a Slovak session — the one known word that adds one or two of them, "nastenná" → "nástenná", "vaš" → "váš" (`_fix_diacritics`, `_diacritic_fix`; only adding: removing or moving one turned Czech and correct forms into other words, and English slipped into the reply is left alone), the recycling-fee mention, Cyrillic letters transliterated, CJK characters and citations of non-existent files removed, outdoor placement options (balkón, fasáda, strecha, záhrada, dvor, garáž…) — a list of them in brackets removed, a sentence naming two or more replaced by the sentence that the PM assesses the placement at the free site visit, once per reply and dropped when the reply already says so (`_replace_placement_lists`: a cut sentence used to leave the next one, "Na základe toho…", pointing at nothing), "s DPH a recyklačným poplatkom" / "s DPH a poplatkami" rewritten to "s DPH, bez recyklačného poplatku" — the fee is always on top — "vrátane recyklačných poplatkov" rewritten to "bez recyklačného poplatku" (`_INCL_FEE_RE`), "zahŕňa aj recyklačný poplatok" to "je bez…" and "recyklačný poplatok je v cene" to "…sa účtuje navyše" (`_FEE_INCLUDED_VERB_RE`, `_FEE_IN_PRICE_RE`), a seasonal wish removed (`_SEASON_WISH_RE`: "príjemné leto" in November), "Lead je zapísaný" to "Vaša žiadosť je zapísaná" (`_LEAD_WRITTEN_RE`; cut as a leak, it took the callback with it), a complaint reply stripped of an opening "Výborne" and of any new-unit offer (`_complaint_tone`), a sentence without figures that the previous reply said word for word removed, and on `odpoved`, `pokracuj`, `kontakt` and `nova-ponuka` the recycling-fee and installation explanations once an earlier reply gave them, unless the customer asks about them — "namontovať" and "termín" count as asking (`_INSTALL_ASK_RE`) — and a sentence that only points back at a dropped one goes with it ("Presnú sumu…", "Jej cenu…", "…rovnako on", `_FOLLOW_UP_RE`) (`_drop_repeats`, `repeat_dropped`; never the consent question or the whole reply), "Ceny sú z ponukového nástroja" / "Všetky sumy sú zo systému `quote`" removed (`_drop_tool_source`), "Ďakujem, pani …, máme vaše údaje" and "Ďakujem, máme meno a telefónne číslo" before consent turned into "Ďakujem, pani …, za vaše údaje" / "Ďakujem za meno a telefónne číslo" (`_fix_have_data`, `_HAVE_ITEMS_RE`; 4 of the 7 `slub-bez-suhlasu` rewrites of the API run of e695916, all complaints), the bot's own feminine forms made masculine — "rada pomôžem", "nepotvrdila som" (`_fix_self_gender`), for a customer who tyká, the stray vykanie of a mixed reply — "vám", "vás", "vaša", "chcete" — turned into tykanie, the consent question excepted (`_fix_mixed_address`; `oslovenie-mix` was the top rewrite, 10 of 56, in the API run of 9657449), "(približne)" after an amount removed, and the safety steps ("volajte 112", "ističom") removed when the customer reported no danger (`_drop_unprompted_safety`), "alebo zavolajte" removed while `COMPANY_PHONE` is empty, "728.10 €", "1217.7 €", "2520 €" and "4 775,4 €" written the Slovak way, a repeated "Dobrý deň" removed, the phrases the model repeats (`_SK_TYPOS`: typos, "záväznú ponuku" → "cenovú ponuku", "Osobne by som vybral" → "Odporúčam", "necenujem" → "nenaceňujem", "kľudne" → "pokojne"), and a Wi-Fi claim under a series without Wi-Fi as standard — a question about Wi-Fi claims nothing (`_fix_wifi_claims`, `_series_without_wifi`: SEIYA, SEIYA Classic 2, the Toshiba console — the nearest series named on that line or above, reset by another brand or two series) completed with "— iba s voliteľným Wi-Fi modulom za príplatok", a "v základe" on that line removed), otherwise only logged. Each of these rules exists because an eval run or a live demo conversation caught the model doing it. The conversation history stores exactly the text that was shown. `_drop_narration` / `_NARRATION_RE` now only decide whether a bookkeeping reply is complete (narration = an announced next step, so the loop continues). Only then is the text emitted as a single `token` event, so a rejected draft never reaches the browser. Don't reintroduce token streaming without keeping that guarantee.

## Added 2026-11-11

- **dB figures** (`nepodlozene-udaje`, HARD). Every dB figure in a reply, both ends of a range ("26 až 41 dB", "19/42 dB") included, must be a noise value a tool returned in the conversation (`_tool_noise_values()`: values under a noise / sound / `_dB` key, or a figure written with "dB") or one the customer typed. A figure under a named series must also be that series' own (`_wrong_noise_figures()`, `_series_noise_values()`: every size and both units of the series in the price lists, single- and multi-split, with the acoustics tables), read per line, heading and table column as `_series_fragments()` does; a flagged table row is cut whole. A difference ("o 10 dB tichšia", `_DB_DIFFERENCE_RE`) is no figure. Before this, any two-digit number anywhere in a tool result passed and a range's lower bound wasn't read. The "wrong dB figures" the AI review reported in the A/B of 2026-11-09 (c16, c20) were correct: the review saw only the first 300 characters of a five-size lookup (fixed in `eval/`, see docs/testing.md).
- **A callback deadline** (`termin-slub`, HARD, in `_DATE_PROMISE_RE`): "zavoláme vám zajtra", "ozve sa do 24 hodín", "within two days". "Do 1 pracovného dňa" is the company's own commitment (the handoff rule, the PM brief) and passes.
- **The rest of a budget called money for the installation** (`montaz-zvysok`, HARD, `_LEFT_FOR_INSTALL_RE`): "Z 1 500 € vám teda pri Samsungu zostane 507,02 € na montáž a poplatok" (QA 2026-11-09, F5). The amount passes the provenance check but reads as an installation budget.
- **"Cenovo najvýhodnejšia", "najvýhodnejšia cena", "najdostupnejšia"** count as "the cheapest" (`_CHEAPEST_WORDS`; "najvýhodnejšia" alone may mean the best value and doesn't), and `_false_cheapest_claim` now also reads the quote's own `not_the_cheapest` field: a quote with a `cheaper_alternative` carries no fit warning, so SEIYA Classic 2 was called the best-priced set while its own quote said AR35 was cheaper (QA 2026-11-09, F8, p08).
- **Announcing its own answer plan** — "Odpoviem na obe otázky priamo a stručne a potom sa spýtam…" — is `interne-info` (red team of 2026-11-10, b03).
- **A request for another language** (`_LANGUAGE_REQUEST_RE`: "Could you please write in English?", "po anglicky", "do you speak…") gets a code-written answer: `_LANGUAGE_REQUEST_REPLY` ("Odpovedám iba po slovensky. Budem písať krátkymi a jednoduchými vetami."; only its second sentence when `_OTHER_LANGUAGE_NOTE` is prepended in the same reply), and the model's own sentences about languages are dropped (`_answer_language_request`, event `language_request_answered`). It had answered "Rozumiem, že anglická verzia je pre vás ťažšia" (QA 2026-11-09, F8, p03).
