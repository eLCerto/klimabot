# Exploratory QA: customer satisfaction on the real API

**Date:** 2026-11-01. **Commit:** `0e0e449`, the deployed version, frozen in an export while another session changed the working tree.
**Model:** `claude-haiku-5-5`, thinking off, production spend caps on, real Anthropic API.
**Scope:** 10 conversations, 51 customer turns. **Cost:** $0.14. No API errors.

**Method:** I played ten customers with a goal and a mood, read every reply, and wrote each next message in reaction to it. Claims were checked against `knowledge/`. Causes marked *reproduced* were replayed against the code with no model call.

**Transcripts:** `eval/runs/qa_csat_2026-11-01/` (gitignored): `conversations/<id>.json`, plus logs and briefs. Line numbers below refer to `0e0e449`.

## Verdict

**Single-room buyers are mostly served well. Multi-room buyers, English speakers and unhappy customers are not.** The average score was **2.5 out of 5**, and 3 of 10 customers would leave satisfied.

- **The main problem is effort.** In 7 of 10 conversations the customer had to repeat a question. The most-repeated case was a 71-year-old customer who asked for the price four times.
- **Two bugs blocked or nearly lost sales.** Both are small parsing errors: a family with a normal three-room flat never got a price, and one customer needed five turns to get one.
- **Hard guarantees held.** Prices came from `quote`, no installation price was given, and all 5 leads passed the GDPR audit. The damage is in understanding, advice and honesty about what happens next.

## P0: lost sales and broken trust

### C01. A real room is dropped from the room list (q05)

"Spálňu 14 m2, detskú izbu 11 m2 a obývačku 24 m2" should have produced a multi-split offer. The fit check said the largest room was 14 m² and refused every system as oversized.

**Cause (reproduced):** `_customer_rooms` (`bot.py:1025`) treats the largest number as a total when it's within 1 m² of the sum of the others. 14 + 11 = 25, so the 24 m² living room vanished. The customer never mentioned a total.

| Customer wrote | Rooms read |
|---|---|
| 14, 11 and 24 m² | 11, 14 |
| 10, 15 and 25 m² | 10, 15 |
| "dve izby, 14 a 15 m2" | 14 |
| "spálňa 20 m2 a obývačka 21 m2" | 20 |

Two rooms of similar size always hit this. That is one of the most common flats there is.

### C02. Refused quotes use up the budget and leave a dead conversation (q05)

C01 turned into a loop. The bot made 10 refused `quote` attempts and repeated the same two multi-split lookups, each pair adding about 18,000 tokens. Context grew from 18,718 to 116,077 tokens over two turns, costing $0.038.

**Cause (reproduced):** `budget_for_next_call` (`bot.py:7562`) estimates the next call from the context size. At 116k tokens the worst case is about $0.016, so two calls no longer fit under $0.07. At 55 % of the cap, every later turn was final-only with tools off, which made a price impossible. No landing message fires because a final-only turn costs just $0.0013.

The customer got this:

> **Customer:** "Už druhýkrát sa pýtam na cenu a stále nič."
> **Bot:** "Mrzí ma, že nacenenie stále nefunguje. Skúsim to znova s oboma zostavami, najprv Samsung." *(nothing follows)*
>
> **Customer:** "Haló? Tak to skúste."
> **Bot:** "…teraz skúsim obe zostavy s vnútornými jednotkami presne podľa veľkostí miestností." *(nothing follows)*

Fixing C01 removes this trigger, not the weakness. Any refused-quote loop can do the same, and q10 reached $0.03 without C01. Three fixes are needed: stop repeated identical lookups, limit retries of a refused quote, and let code write an honest closing when an offer move can no longer call a tool.

### C03. Internal reasoning shown to customers (q09, q05)

F04 was fixed for Slovak phrasing only. Every English line below went through the gate.

| Reply | Text shown |
|---|---|
| q09 turn 3 | "The customer's last message wasn't in this turn, so I'll respond to the open question from before…" |
| q09 turn 3 | "The tool did not accept this combination because of how it checks the sizing against your room list" |
| q09 turn 4 | "Its message said that a 3.5 kW unit is oversized for a 28 m² room, and it flagged the set as a mismatch." |
| q09 turns 1, 2 | "Prices are from our quote tool" |
| q05 turn 1 | "Čo viem spoľahlivo: pre byt s tromi izbami…" |
| q05 turn 3 | "Mrzí ma, že nacenenie stále nefunguje." |

The customer asked what "the tool did not accept" meant, and the bot explained its own tool at more length.

**Cause (reproduced):** `_INTERNAL_LEAK_RE` (`bot.py:5836`) has `nástroj` but not `tool`, and `zákazník` but not `the customer`. All six lines above pass it. Word lists have now missed new wordings twice, so a separate check for customer-facing text may be worth considering.

### C04. "Len do spálne" isn't understood (q01)

A customer with a two-room flat said clearly that only the bedroom matters. The bot kept asking.

| Turn | Customer | Bot |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | "…v spálni, tá bude mať tak 14 m2, obývačku nechám tak… Koľko ma to bude stáť?" | "…chcete klimatizáciu len do spálne, alebo aj do obývačky?" |
| 3 | "Len do spálne, to som už písala… koľko to bude stáť?" | "…je spálňa 14 m² jediná miestnosť…?" |
| 4 | "…len tá jedna spálňa… píšem to tretíkrát." | "Zatiaľ vám cenu ešte nemôžem potvrdiť…" and a new question about ceiling height and south windows |
| 5 | "Áno, len spálňa, 14 m2, okno je na východ. Cena?" | The price, at last |

In turn 4 the model tried to quote and code refused it twice (`discovery_quote`).

**Cause (reproduced):** `_ROOMS_ANSWERED_RE` (`bot.py:6890`) needs "len" directly before the room word. "Len do spálne" fails, and so does every "iba" form. There's also no limit on how often `zisti-izby` repeats. It should ask once, as the whole-house branch already does.

### C05. Advice that costs the customer money or wrongly rules a product out

**q10, turn 3.** The customer asked for the cheapest option for three bedrooms. The bot said three sets cost "približne trojnásobok" of €895.56, "takže pri troch spálňach sa oplatí multi-split". Its own figures say otherwise: three sets cost €2,686.69 and the multi-split €3,564.54, so the advice costs **€877.85 more**. The customer caught it: "ale 3x 895 je menej ako 3564 nie?" The bot then apologised correctly.

**q04, turn 2.** With a €900 budget: "Samotné jednotky vychádzajú nad rozpočet". The offer was €1,095.93 and €1,760.13. `quote` had returned the AR35 at €895.56 as `cheaper_alternative`, which fits. The bot ignored that until challenged.

**Cause:** in both cases `quote` said the right thing and the model didn't use it. The gate checks the word "najlacnejší", but not a claim of value or fit. In q10 the code that leads with separate sets runs only on `ponuka`, not on `nova-ponuka` (`bot.py:7054`).

### C06. The bot says something was passed on or noted, and nothing was

**q06.** "Váš prípad bol už odovzdaný nášmu tímu", then "Váš prípad je už zaznamenaný, takže sa nemusíte obávať, že sa začína od nuly." The record is one line in `handoff_queue.jsonl`, which nothing in the repo reads or mails. The bot gave this reassurance to a customer who said nobody had answered for two weeks. The gate caught "je zaznamenaná" in turn 4, but not these wordings.

**q02.** "Ďakujem, zapíšem si, že volať máme po 17:00." The note exists in no brief and in `leads.jsonl` nowhere. On `po-zapise` the model called no tool. The PM will call during the day, as the customer asked them not to.

## P1: wrong answers and friction

### C07. Direct questions are skipped on offer moves

Once the move is `ponuka`, the directive says to price two options, and the customer's own question falls away.

| Conversation | Asked | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| q08 turn 2 | "Koľko dB to má? Je to tichšie ako chladnička? Bude to počuť susedov?" | Two priced options, none of the three answered |
| q10 turns 1, 2 | "Da sa tym kurit?" | Not answered. Turn 3: "treti krat sa pytam". The answer was a hedge, though the rules say every model heats |
| q09 turn 2 | "Do I need the landlord's permission?" | Skipped. Turn 3: "You didn't answer about the landlord." |
| q03 turn 1 | "Čo je lepšie a prečo?" | A priorities question instead |
| q01 turn 6 | "Zavolá mi niekto?" | "Otázku, ktorú vám teraz položíme, nájdete pod touto správou." |
| q07 turn 2 | "S recyklačným poplatkom alebo bez?" | Listed as a line, with no answer to the question |

The bot recovers when challenged, but the customer has to challenge it.

### C08. False noise facts behind a purchase decision

- **q03:** the customer's first priority was quiet. The bot said Toshiba is "mierne lepšia" and that the Samsung has no such values in the data, then recommended Toshiba, which the customer bought. `rac.json` lists the WindFree Premiere 3.5 kW at **16/46 dB**, against the Toshiba's 23 dB at low speed. **Likely cause:** `_brand_size_sets` returns no noise fields, so the model saw figures for one brand only and didn't look up the other.
- **q08:** "Vonkajšia jednotka má 41 dB(A)" for HAORI. 41 is the indoor unit at high speed. The outdoor figure in `ras_single.json` is 44 dB. The conclusion that it's quieter outside than AR35 (55 dB) still holds, but `lookup_product` gives no Toshiba outdoor noise at all, and turn 4 recommended SEIYA as quiet outside without a number (the price list gives 47 dB).

### C09. Offers that don't fit the room

- **q01:** a 3.5 kW set for 14 m², described as "výkon zvolený pre plochu 14 m²". **Reproduced:** "byt v Trnave, asi 55 m2" isn't read as a total, so 55 m² counts as a room and the oversizing check doesn't fire.
- **q09 turn 1:** one 4.2 kW set offered for a two-room flat. **Reproduced:** "(about 45 m2 total)" is read as one room, and `_FLAT_ROOMS_RE` knows no English.
- **q09 turns 2 to 4:** 2.5 kW for a 28 m² room, then "sized for rooms up to 15 m²", which contradicts the glossary's 25 m². The fit check refused 3.5 kW even with sun. The uncommitted sizing change has a test expecting 28 m² with 3.5 kW to pass. I did not test that live.
- **q10:** 1.5 kW for a 16 m² bedroom called "dostatočná", under the 1.6 kW rule and inside the undersizing allowance.

### C10. Budget and price wishes missed, premium offered by default

- **Reproduced:** "maximálne 900 eur… Zmestím sa do toho?" isn't a price question, so q04 got a priorities question instead of an answer.
- **Reproduced:** "Ide mi hlavne o cenu" isn't a cheap wish, so the second offer was €1,760 on a €900 budget.
- **q05, q10:** no one asked for premium, yet the first multi-split offer was WindFree Premiere Plus and SHORAI CURVE. q10 saw €4,959, and the cheapest multi-split turned out to be €3,564.

### C11. Complaint handling (q06)

- **Reproduced:** "Klimatizácia, ktorú ste mi minulý rok montovali, mi tečie na stenu" isn't matched by `_COMPLAINT_RE` or `_OUR_INSTALL_RE`. The first reply ended by asking an angry customer what space they want to cool.
- **Cold or invented replies:** "Adresu prevádzky vám nedám", and "reklamácie… rieši náš tím výhradne cez e-mail", which no rule says.
- **No way forward:** the customer said the e-mail failed twice, and the bot gave the same address in every reply. In this run `COMPANY_PHONE` was empty, as it is in `.env`. Whether production sets it isn't something I could see.

### C12. An explicit "Súhlasím" is rejected (q04)

The customer wrote name, phone, e-mail, town and "Súhlasím". One message had passed since the consent question, so code refused it and replied "potrebujeme vaše výslovné „áno"". The customer: "Veď som napísal že súhlasím." This follows the rule exactly, but it reads as absurd. See the owner decisions. The same message also got `nova-ponuka` instead of `kontakt`, because "nech príde obhliadka na ten AR35" names a series but matches no choice pattern.

### C13. Consent paragraph pushed on undecided customers

The fix for F21 tells the model not to ask yet, and the model complies: "Ak sa rozhodnete, požiadam vás o meno…" (q08 turn 4). `_asks_contact` reads that as a request and appends the full consent question. The same happened on q04 turn 3 and q09 turn 4.

### C14. Wrong statement about the recycling fee (q07)

"Ponuku bez DPH, vrátane recyklačných poplatkov." The fee is always on top. The cleanup catches "s DPH a recyklačným poplatkom", but not this wording, and the bot never corrected it even when the customer asked.

## P2: polish

### C15. Invented statements

- **q02:** "Zrušenie vybavíme… nevznikajú vám žiadne záväzky" for an "objednávka". The handoff rule forbids that word, and the bot invented the cancellation terms.
- **q10:** "pes si na ňu väčšinou rýchlo zvykne", twice, with no source.
- **q03:** "predpríprava… výrazne urýchli inštaláciu", and advice to listen to both units, with nowhere to do so.

### C16. Tone and language

- **q10:** tykanie and vykanie mixed, logged on 4 of 6 replies. Soft only, so nothing fixes it.
- **Grammatical gender changes within a chat:** "rada pomôžem" next to "aby som vedel" (q10), "nepotvrdila" and "mal som" (q10).
- **q01, for a senior customer:** "…je pre nás jasný, a preto vám k tomu zatiaľ len krátko odpoviem."
- **q03:** "filtrácia najvyššej triedy v rade (poradie 1)". The customer had to ask what it means.
- **q05 turn 2:** the reply opens with "Preto vám zatiaľ nemôžem…" after a cut, the F11 pattern again.
- **q05, q09:** "which brand" is asked after all. On the last attempt it's only a soft violation.

### C17. PM brief gaps

In q02 the phone field holds the e-mail too, and "Iné modely" reads "Samsung AR35 WIFI 2, 6 kW" because the state list split on the decimal comma.

## What worked

- **Simple sales are good.** q02 went from question to lead in three turns, and q07 needed no fixing: it refused a cassette without a ceiling and proposed an under-ceiling unit so nothing blows on clients.
- **Pressure held.** No installation figure under direct pushing (q04). All amounts traced to `quote`.
- **GDPR:** 5 leads, 5 OK, 0 violations in the audit. Refusals in q09 and q10 stored no personal data.
- **Graceful exits.** q08 left with no pressure and a recap. q09 and q10 got a useful summary and the contact.
- **Honest recovery.** When caught out (q04, q08, q10) the bot admitted it and gave the right answer.
- **The gate stopped real errors:** contact requests in a complaint, false "cheapest" labels, and an unquoted total.
- **Speed.** A median of 4.3 s feels instant, and the expensive turn was still only 26 s.

## Owner decisions

1. **Where does a complaint go?** Today it's one line in a file nothing reads, while the bot tells customers it was handed over. It needs a real route, plus a phone number and an address if you want to give them. Until then, the bot shouldn't claim a handover at all.
2. **Should "Súhlasím" count if one message passed since the question?** This is a DPO question. The wording is still marked as a draft.
3. **Does English stay?** If it does, the gate must cover it. q09 was the worst experience of the run and the only English conversation.
4. **What may the bot say when a tenant asks about permission?** The rule stops the bot raising permits, but a customer asked directly. The English answer here (check your lease, ask your landlord) seemed right. Slovak wasn't tested.
5. **Installation price.** q04 and q10 both pushed for a rough figure, and q04 was reluctant to have a technician come out without one. I'm not suggesting changing the rule, but it does cost satisfaction.

## Suggested fix order

1. **C01:** infer a total only when the customer says so. It unblocks multi-room sales.
2. **C04:** accept "len do", "iba" and "jedna", and ask about rooms once.
3. **C03:** extend the gate to English.
4. **C06:** stop false handover claims, and store notes given after a lead.
5. **C02:** limit refused-quote retries and repeated lookups, and let code write the closing when tools are off.
6. **C07, C10:** answer the question first on offer moves, and recognise budget and price wishes.
7. **C05, C08:** carry separate sets into `nova-ponuka`, and return noise data for both brands and both units.
8. **C09 to C17:** the rest.

## Limits of this run

- **Ten conversations, each run once.** Findings show what can happen, not how often. Scores are one tester's judgement.
- **I kept customers going longer than real ones would stay.** q01's lead would probably not exist, so the 5 of 9 sales conversations ending in a lead is optimistic.
- **Frozen at `0e0e449`.** The uncommitted hotel and sizing changes were not tested live. I reran the room-parsing checks on them, and C01 and C04 are unchanged.
- **Pipeline only.** No browser, so the price card, consent buttons and web limits weren't exercised. Lead mail was off.
- **Not reached:** conversation summarising, and a real `limit_reached` landing.
- **Contact details** were the defaults: `info@klimabot.example`, no phone.
- **No fixes were made or replayed.** The code wasn't changed.
