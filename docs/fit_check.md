# Fit check and code-picked offers

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-11 so the file every session loads stays short; CLAUDE.md keeps a summary and points here. This is the full reference: what `quote`'s fit check refuses or warns about, the code-picked and re-picked offers, and how the customer's areas and rooms are read. Every rule carries the reason and the date it was added. Keep it current with `bot.py`: a new rule gets its line here, with why and when.

**Fit check** (`_fit_check`, thresholds in `sizing_policy.json`; rules point there instead of restating numbers): the place and the heat load are read from what the customer typed, never from model-written state. A server room, salon or medical practice named by the customer + residential lines is an **error**; a server room also needs ≥ 2 independent systems (no Twin/multi), each with cooling ≥ `capacity_factor_per_system` × the stated kW, plus the brand's failover controller. Cassettes are refused when the customer said there is no suspended ceiling. The model may pass `override_reason` when the customer insists, which turns these errors into `fit_warnings`. Technical impossibilities are never overridable (`_commercial_combo_errors`, and `_accessory_errors`: a cassette without a decorative panel, or a cassette, ducted, ceiling or Toshiba RAV floor-standing unit without a controller — wall, console and other floor-standing units come with a remote; `quote` then says override_reason won't bypass it): more commercial (RAV/CAC) indoor units than outdoor units without a Twin/Triple kit or refnet, or indoor capacity far above the outdoor unit's. When the model doesn't pass `budget_eur`, `quote` takes the budget from the customer's own words (`_customer_budget`; "zmestí sa do tých 1800e" names one too); a budget named after the offers makes code re-run the last two offers' quotes before the first call (`_with_budget_requote`), so the reply has their `budget.status`, and a budget that includes installation lets the reply say only that the units fit (`_budget_note`); a `budget_eur` the customer never typed (±1 € of `_customer_amounts()`) is ignored (QA 2026-11-03: "Pri vašom rozpočte 1 000 €" to a customer who named none). An oversizing error names the smaller set's codes. A salon or medical practice with a commercial wall unit is an (overridable) error: it blows at clients, the rule prescribes an under-ceiling unit (or a cassette under a suspended ceiling). On the offer moves `_commercial_units_note` tells the model where those units are ("Toshiba podstropná <kW>"). Warnings also cover keyword-inferred commercial places (a shop, gastro, fitness; not an office: an office of any size, open space included, gets wall sets like a flat and no warning — the owner, 2026-11-09, after a 30 m² office was told its residential prices weren't recommended and got no price, F4 of `docs/qa_exploratory_2026-11-09.md`; `office_words` only keep Wi-Fi and design out of its priorities question), undersizing against m², a Wi-Fi wish on a set without Wi-Fi as standard (with the cheapest same-size set that has it, from the series' `key_features`), and a "cheap" wish when a cheaper suitable set exists (`_cheapest_set`: enough kW for the m², Wi-Fi and a single named brand respected). Wishes are matched on diacritic-folded text and not when negated (`_wished`: "neriešim wifi", "nechcem najlacnejší"; "základný rad" / "obyčajná" / "pomer cena/výkon" / "finančne menej náročné" / "nízka cena" / "najnižšia cena" (the bot's own priorities option) / "nie drahá" count as cheap). One set bigger than the room needs is an (overridable) error when the same series has a smaller size that meets the rule in full, m² × `kw_per_m2` (the owner, 2026-11-01: `undersize_ratio` is only the lower limit, and code-picked offers go down to it only for a customer who wants the cheapest, `_offer_min_kw`) — as a warning the model ignored it. The same goes for a multi-split wall unit bigger than the largest room the customer named needs; when the customer names two or more room sizes, a largest indoor unit too small for the largest room is an (overridable) error (a total the customer calls one — "spolu 54 m²", "54 m2 total", the whole flat or house — "Byt v Trnave, asi 55 m2" — is no room, `_M2_TOTAL_RE`, `_M2_TOTAL_AFTER_RE`; the sum of the others counts only when that number opens the list on its own, `_opens_with_bare_area`: 14, 11 and 24 m² lost the living room and every multi-split was refused; a single size next to a total is a room's, so "Každá izba má 20 M2" sizes the units and the oversizing checks), and a Wi-Fi wish on a multi-split wall unit without Wi-Fi as standard is a warning naming the cheapest same-size one with it (`_unit_has_wifi`: the multi-split price lists have no `key_features`, the same series' single-split sets do; `_wall_multi_units`). "20mm2", "30m" (not pipe or height) and Cyrillic "м2" count as m² (`_CUSTOMER_M2_RE`); read areas through `_customer_m2()`, which takes a list whole ("25, 15 a 12 m2", `_M2_LIST_RE`; the unit stands only after the last number), counts a bare number beside a room word when another area with a unit was stated ("kuchyňa má 15m obývačka 20", `_ROOM_BARE_M2_RE`), or, with no unit anywhere, when two rooms have one ("spalna 12 a obyvacka 20") or a linking word stands before it ("spálňa má 14", `_AREA_LINK_RE`), 6–80 only — the QA of 2026-11-09 lost a customer who never typed "m2" (five turns on `zisti-priestor`) and adds up a kitchen and living room the customer calls one space ("sú spojené", "otvorená kuchyňa", `_OPEN_PLAN_RE`, `_open_plan_parts`: the live conversation of 2026-11-02 read 15 m² for a 35 m² space, refused every right size as oversized and sold 2.5 kW), and rooms through `_customer_rooms()`. An open-plan space is one room: a set under `undersize_ratio` of it is an (overridable) error, not a warning (unless the customer asked for that size — below), and the server context says the PM confirms the final size at the site visit, because the kitchen adds heat (the sizing rule's "bigger size or a site visit"). A black or wood set when the customer named no colour or design and typed no code is an (overridable) error naming the white set of that size (`_COLOUR_WISH_RE`). A series the customer named in any message (`_named_series`: the `_SERIES_PATTERNS` keys, "WindFree" alone for its family; not when a code is typed, which the model quotes) is picked by code too: its smallest white set covering the room, one option per series named, up to two, the family as one option (its cheapest series); one priced named option completes the offer (`tool_choice` auto), and `dve-moznosti` (`_single_first_offer`) reads every message for the named series, since "Samsung WindFree" often comes a message before the size (8 of the 22 offers code didn't price in the A/B of 2026-11-07). `nova-ponuka` is priced ahead too: a series named after an offer ("A čo HAORI 3,5 kW?", `_named_variant_sets`: its white wall set at the kW typed in that message, else the smallest covering the room; one priced option completes the turn; a typed code is left to the model), and, when the customer named nothing new (`_new_variant_prerun`: the two cheapest sets with a wished feature the offers lack, from `_feature_set_rows`, or the cheapest room sets of one brand plus one multi-split, `_cheapest_rooms_plan`); with two priced quotes its first call is auto. A wish said after an offer that changes what code picks — the best filtration, "the best", Wi-Fi, a quieter set — or a multi-split asked for by name, not inside a question about the offers shown ("A má Wi-Fi?"), is `nova-ponuka` too (`_repick_after_offer`): code re-runs the first offer's picks (`_suggested_multis` for 2–5 rooms, else `_suggested_sets(after_offer=True)`), drops those already quoted and prices the rest; nothing new to pick leaves the move as it was (live 2026-11-09: "potrebujem čistenie vzduchu čo najlepšie" after two multi-splits was `odpoved`, the model priced separate sets, and the customer asked why the multi-split was gone). A first offer to a customer who stated no priority (`_preferences_stated()`; the bot asking counts for `_preferences_known()` only) opens with "vybral som možnosti podľa ceny" and closes asking whether one suits or something else matters. After an offer, a customer who hands the choice to the bot — "vyberte mi", "čo by ste zobrali vy", "je mi to jedno", "which would you recommend" (`_DELEGATE_RE`) — gets `_RECOMMEND_TEXT`: one option, the one that fits what they said (else the cheaper), why in a sentence or two, its price, and whether they want it, without the second option again (`odpoved`, or on top of a re-pick or a comparison; c05 of the API run of e695916 got both options again). On the comparison `odpoved`, "o koľko" / "rozdiel" answered with no difference between two quoted totals gets quote's own `reads` sentence from code, before the closing question (`_difference_text`, `difference_appended`; d01). "Prečo si mi (ne)ponúkol…" (`_WHY_OFFERED_RE`) gets a `_questions_note` fact: explain from the offers, no "máte pravdu" or "mal som…" (the live replies admitted faults that weren't). Code-picked multi-splits come with a `lookup_product` '<series> <kW>' per option in the prerun (`_multi_series_lookups`; not with a quiet wish, which looks up each unit), and a question about the offers' features, filters, dB, SEER or Wi-Fi (`_SPEC_QUESTION_RE`, `_NOISE_ASK_RE`, `_FEATURE_ASK_RE`) on `odpoved` gets the quoted series looked up by code, those no earlier round looked up (`_quoted_series_lookups`): the model made those calls itself, one more call per turn (live 2026-11-09: 9.3 and 21.5 s). A wish put as a request ("Máte niečo s najlepšou filtráciou?", `_REQUEST_ASK_RE`) re-picks like one without a question mark. The new-offer moves carry the power each room needs by `kw_per_m2` and ask for one sentence on why a size changed (`_sizing_note`; q09 and d03 jumped sizes unexplained). `_EXACT_AMOUNTS` allows at most three features per option, those that match the wishes, and `_drop_repeats` drops the fee and installation explanations on `nova-ponuka` too. **Quiet and cheaper re-picks** (F3 and F7 of the QA of 2026-11-09): a quieter set asked for after an offer (`_QUIET_WISH_RE` in `_REPICK_WISH_RES`; "Tá Toshiba sa mi páči, ale nemáte niečo ešte tichšie?" got "Tichšiu… som nenašiel" while WindFree has 16 dB) makes code pick the quietest white wall sets quieter than every set quoted so far (`_suggested_sets(quiet=True)`, `_quiet_db`: the indoor unit's `indoor_noise_min_dB`; another brand when the wanted one has none quieter, and `_picks_text` says so); nothing quieter left gives no pick. "Lacnejšie" with a filtration wish in the same message after an offer ("Máte niečo lacnejšie, ale stále s dobrou filtráciou?" got the same Avant S2 again) picks per brand the set cheaper than that brand's quoted one (catalog `set_price_EUR`, `_quoted_sets`), best filtration first among tiers 1–3 (`cheaper_than`; the owner: the cheaper one), and when none of them is tier 1 the directive says no cheaper best-filtration set exists. After an offer one new pick is enough. A quiet wish anywhere in the customer's words puts `_quiet_ranking_text()` into the server context: the white wall series by the indoor unit's dB at its lowest speed, quietest first, each series' smallest set for the room, grouped by dB, with "don't say nothing is quieter when the ranking has a quieter one, of any brand". A quiet wish (`_QUIET_WISH_RE`) makes `ponuka` ask for each option's dB from `lookup_product`; it doesn't stop code from picking (the owner, 2026-11-08: the cheapest set per brand, as without it, with the dB shown — the series lookups of `_picks_prerun` carry both units' dB, and a code-picked multi-split adds a `lookup_product` per unit; `_TASTE_WISH_RE`, which keeps the pick with the model, is looks only — design, colour, black, wood, textile, "krajšie"). "Tichá" left the pick to the model in 6 of the 22 offer turns code didn't price in the A/B of 2026-11-07, 33 s against 15 s at the median. A design wish (`_DESIGN_WISH_RE`) puts the colours and a note to offer them into the server context; design named first (`_DESIGN_FIRST_RE`, "skôr dizajn… nech to nestojí majland") beats a cheap wish, so code picks no cheapest white sets. Fewer indoor units than rooms is one of several systems, so the largest room doesn't size it. The cheapest suitable set is the one for the largest room the quoted set covers, and a cheap wish on a multi-split or commercial units gets a warning when separate sets cost clearly less (`_cheaper_separate_sets`). N identical multi-split systems are an (overridable) error when one outdoor unit of the price list takes every indoor unit (`_multi_outdoors`, check_compatibility's limits: four rooms got 2× AJ050 although AJ080 takes four), and an incompatible multi-split names the outdoor units that would take them. One outdoor unit bigger than the smallest one with at least the indoor units' total cooling is an (overridable) error too. **A feature the customer asks for** (`_FEATURE_WISHES`: ionizátor / plazma, WindFree, Freeze Wash, Ultra Pure, HADA, Energy Monitoring, radar; matched in the customer's words and in the series' `key_features`, where "Bez …" and optional ones don't count): code-picked sets and multi-split units and every cheapest-set lookup keep only series that have it — two series of one brand when only that brand has it — and a set or wall unit without it is an (overridable) error naming the cheapest set of that size with it. After an offer without it, the wish is `nova-ponuka` with those sets (`_feature_sets_text`). The live conversation of 2026-11-03 asked for "čo najlacnejšiu a s ionizátorom", got the two cheapest sets without one and then HAORI over the cheaper SHORAI CURVE. **Undersizing the customer insists on** (the owner, 2026-11-03): a set too small for the room is only a warning when the customer typed its kW or its code — an open-plan space and a multi-split's largest room included — and `quote` returns `undersized` ({m2, need_kW, kW}). Code-picked offers follow a smaller kW the customer typed (`_offer_min_kw`: "Chcem 2,5 kW, väčšiu nechcem" for 50 m² got only 5 kW sets in the live check). A reply that shows an undersized set's price without saying so gets the warning from code, once per set (`_undersize_text`, `undersize_warning_added`), and if the customer still wants it the lead is written as usual: the PM record opens "Záujem" with "⚠ Poddimenzované" for the chosen set. Oversizing errors give way to `override_reason` only when the customer asked for more power or a reserve, typed a kW figure or a code (`_BIGGER_WISH_RE`) — the model used to override them with "the customer wants this series". The indoor unit type: a wall unit is the standard, so a residential console, cassette or ducted unit (`_NON_WALL_PATH_RE` on the section path) is an (overridable) error unless the customer asked for that type (`_INDOOR_TYPE_WISH_RE`) or typed its code; discovery asks for the type with wall-mounted as the recommendation. "The best AC" (`_BEST_WISH_RE`: "najlepšiu", "prémiovú", "špičkovú", not "najlepší pomer cena/výkon" nor "čo najlepšiu cenu", which is a cheap wish like "len cena" and "cena je pre mňa hlavná") means the best filtration and the most features (the owner, 2026-10-21): the server context asks for tier-1 series only, one per brand, with no cheaper second option, `quote` warns about a tier-2+ set or multi-split wall unit, and `cheaper_alternative` stays off. A quality comparison ("kvalitnejšia") also puts the ranking in the server context. Allergy, dust, air quality or a filter wish ("kvalitný filter", "filtrácia", "čistenie vzduchu") in the customer's words (`_ALLERGY_RE`) puts the filtration ranking (`filtration_policy.json`, tiers 1–4, equal within a tier; the model sees the tiers in words only — `_TIER_WORDS`, "najlepšia filtrácia", "o stupeň slabšia"…, and `lookup_product`'s `filtration` field — because "filtráciou triedy 1" echoed from the numbered ranking was the top `interne-info` regeneration of the A/B of 2026-11-06) into the server context and makes `quote` warn about a tier-2+ set, naming each tier-1 series' smallest set that covers the room, with its exact codes, and saying the quote is the weaker series' price (`_best_filtration_sets`; about the same size when no m² is known; a single named brand respected) — the live demo of 2026-10-27 got only names, guessed Avant S2's code, quoted Comfort S2's (AR60F… for AR70F…) and showed its price under the Avant S2 name. `_check_filtration_policy()` stops startup when a residential wall series is unranked or a ranked one no longer exists. Two to five single-split sets in one quote (no commercial place, no multi outdoor) get a warning to price a multi-split too. Fewer indoor units than the 2–5 rooms the customer still wants cooled (`_customer_rooms()`, so rooms dropped don't count) get a warning not to leave a room out unasked: say which rooms the set is for and what about the others, or price every room (q09 of the API run of e695916 dropped the bedroom). A Toshiba multi-split outdoor unit with no more indoor units than outdoor units gets a warning that it only cools (the price list's `combinations_table`). `_canonical_code` also accepts a Toshiba code that differs only in its `-E` / `-E1` / `-E2` revision when a single revision exists (the 2.5 kW HAORI indoor unit is `-E2`, the other sizes `-E1`).
